Edmonton River Valley Fall 2025

April 24, 2026:

Finally getting around to posting these. These are from a bike ride down the Edmonton river valley on October 26th 2025.

Looking north across the high level bridge.


Metro Line train heading southbound over the Dudley B. Menzies Bridge.


Rossdale Power Plant. This is next to the neat looking bridge everyone likes to take pictures of.



Flare at the Gold Bar Wastewater Treatment Plant.



Strathcona Oil Refinery





'Gilead East' - a pharmaceutical plant if google maps is to be believed.


'Air Products Canada Ltd' (grey on the left), probably an atmospheric separation plant. The black building on the right is labeled 'Rio Tinto Alcan Inc.' but that company doesn't mention an Edmonton facility anywhere so that's either outdated or wrong.




'Cloverbar Energy Center', a natural gas fired power plant. Bit sad that's it's not a more efficient combined cycle plant like the one I toured in Toronto.


No idea what this is.


Suncor Energy Edmonton Refinery


A glimpse of the endless soul crushing suburbia that makes up most of Edmonton.
